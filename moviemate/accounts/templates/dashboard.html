Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MovieMate tickets & cinema bookings in Ahmedabad.

Booking is simple! Select your desired movie from the home page, pick your preferred cinema hall in Ahmedabad and showtime date, choose your seat tier (Platinum, Gold, or Silver), and complete payment to instantly receive your digital ticket with QR code.

Click on My Bookings in the top navigation bar. Every confirmed booking contains your live ticket details along with a verified digital QR code that can be scanned at the cinema entrance.

MovieMate is currently operational exclusively in Ahmedabad. We are actively expanding to Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Delhi NCR soon!

We provide three seat tiers depending on the theater setup: Platinum (Recliners & Premium View), Gold (Executive Middle Rows), and Silver (Standard View). Prices are displayed transparently during showtime selection.

Confirmed bookings are locked into the cinema system to guarantee your seat reservation. You can review all your booking details anytime under the My Bookings tab.
